## Story
RCA-010 Partner account type requires a partner price book

Given a new Account with Type = "Partner" and currency GBP
When the account is saved
Then it is created, and an Opportunity created from it uses price book "Partner GBP"
And an Account of Type "Partner" with no Industry is rejected by the validation rule "Partner_Industry_Required"

Expected result: account created; opportunity price book = Partner GBP; negative case returns FIELD_CUSTOM_VALIDATION_EXCEPTION
Data prerequisites: price book "Partner GBP" active; validation rule Partner_Industry_Required active in DEV
