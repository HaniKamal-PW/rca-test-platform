## Story
RCA-013 Accepted quote becomes an activated order

Given an Accepted quote with 10 × SUB-ANALYTICS-PRO at 50.00
When an order is created from the quote and activated
Then the order has one order product with quantity 10 and unit price 50.00, order total 500.00, Status Activated
And the Opportunity stage is "Closed Won"

Expected result: OrderItem qty 10 unit 50.00; Order.TotalAmount 500.00; Order.Status Activated; Opportunity.StageName Closed Won
Data prerequisites: product SUB-ANALYTICS-PRO active in "Standard GBP"; flow "Order Activated → Close Opportunity" active in DEV
