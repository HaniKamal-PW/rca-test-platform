## Story
RCA-023 Mid-term amendment prorates the quantity increase

Given an activated 12-month order of 10 × SUB-ANALYTICS-PRO at 50.00/yr starting on the 1st of this month
When the asset is amended to 15 units 6 months in
Then the amendment quote has one delta line of +5 units and a prorated total of 125.00 (5 × 50.00 × 6/12)
And the asset's current quantity is 15 after the amendment order is activated

Expected result: delta quantity 5; prorated total 125.00; asset CurrentQuantity 15
Data prerequisites: SUB-ANALYTICS-PRO Term Annual in "Standard GBP"; asset lifecycle enabled
