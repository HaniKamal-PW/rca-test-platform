## Story
RCA-011 Synced quote drives opportunity amount

Given an Opportunity in stage Prospecting on a GBP account
When a quote with 10 × SUB-ANALYTICS-PRO at list 50.00 is created and set as the synced quote
Then the Opportunity Amount equals the quote total 500.00
And creating a second quote does not change the Amount until it is synced

Expected result: Opportunity.Amount = 500.00 after sync; unchanged by an unsynced second quote
Data prerequisites: product SUB-ANALYTICS-PRO active in price book "Standard GBP" at 50.00
