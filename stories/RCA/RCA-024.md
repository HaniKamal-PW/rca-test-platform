## Story
RCA-024 Renewal with 5% uplift

Given an activated 12-month order of 10 × SUB-ANALYTICS-PRO at 50.00/yr
When the assets are renewed for 12 months with a 5% uplift
Then the renewal quote has one line, qty 10, net unit price 52.50, total 525.00, and the new term starts the day after the current end date

Expected result: qty 10; netUnitPrice 52.50; total 525.00; start = current end + 1 day
Data prerequisites: SUB-ANALYTICS-PRO Term Annual; renewal uplift allowed on the selling model
