## Story
RCA-012 Discount above 20% requires deal desk approval

Given a quote with a manual discount of 25% on SUB-ANALYTICS-PRO
When the sales rep submits the quote for approval
Then an approval request is pending for the deal desk approver and the quote Status is "In Review"
When the approver approves
Then the quote Status is "Approved" and the quote is locked for line edits
And a quote with a 10% discount needs no approval

Expected result: pending work item exists for persona approver; Status In Review → Approved; 10% case: no work item, Status stays Draft
Data prerequisites: approval process "Quote Discount Approval" active with a 20% threshold; approver user has a login alias RCA_DEV_APPROVER
