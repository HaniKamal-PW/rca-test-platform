## Story
RCA-021 Attribute-based pricing: tier attribute changes the unit price

Given product SUB-ANALYTICS-PRO with attribute "Tier" (Standard | Enterprise), Term Annual, price book "Standard GBP"
When Tier = Standard, qty 1 → net unit price 50.00
When Tier = Enterprise, qty 1 → net unit price 80.00

Expected result: Standard 50.00; Enterprise 80.00
Data prerequisites: attribute Tier on product class Analytics; attribute-based price adjustment configured
